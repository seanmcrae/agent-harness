# Incident response

A Sev1 incident is a full outage or data loss affecting multiple customers.
The on-call engineer is paged within 5 minutes of a Sev1 alert and must acknowledge within 15 minutes.
During a Sev1 incident, the status page is updated at least every 30 minutes until resolution.
A written post-incident review is published to affected customers within 5 business days.
